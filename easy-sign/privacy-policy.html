Easy Sign Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

Easy Sign is designed to sign PDF documents locally on the user's Android device. This policy explains how the app accesses and handles data.

Data processed on the device

Easy Sign may access PDF documents selected by the user, handwritten strokes, saved signature presets, temporary exported PDFs, and recovery drafts. These items are processed locally on the device.

No off-device collection

Easy Sign does not transmit documents, signatures, device identifiers, analytics data, advertising data, or other user data to Easy Sign servers or to third-party servers. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

PDF access

PDF files are opened only after the user selects them through Android's system file picker. Easy Sign does not request broad storage access.

Local security

Saved signature presets and recovery drafts are stored in the app's private storage. Sensitive local catalog and draft data are protected using AES-GCM encryption with keys maintained by Android Keystore. Android backup is disabled for the app.

Exports and sharing

Temporary exported PDFs are stored in the app cache so the user can share them. Sharing occurs only when the user chooses Export and selects a receiving app through Android's share interface. After a file is handed to a receiving app, that app handles the file under its own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Saved signatures remain on the device until the user deletes them, clears Easy Sign's app data, or uninstalls the app. Recovery drafts are local recovery data and are replaced as the current document changes. Temporary export files are cache data and may be removed by Android or when the app's data is cleared.

Third-party software

Easy Sign uses open-source PDF and cryptography libraries for local document processing. The app does not grant itself Internet access for those libraries to transmit user documents or signatures.

Children

Easy Sign does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users because it does not transmit user data off the device.

Changes to this policy

If Easy Sign's data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before those changes are distributed.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact QED at qed.xyz.yt@gmail.com.

Política de Privacidade do Easy Sign

Vigência: 9 de agosto de 2026

O Easy Sign foi projetado para assinar documentos PDF localmente no dispositivo Android do usuário. PDFs selecionados, traços manuscritos, assinaturas salvas, PDFs exportados temporariamente e rascunhos de recuperação são processados no próprio aparelho.

O Easy Sign não transmite documentos, assinaturas, identificadores do dispositivo, dados de análise, publicidade ou outros dados do usuário para servidores do Easy Sign ou de terceiros. O app não exige conta e não contém publicidade nem SDKs de análise.

Os PDFs só são abertos após seleção pelo seletor de arquivos do Android. Assinaturas salvas e rascunhos de recuperação ficam no armazenamento privado do aplicativo e são protegidos com AES-GCM e chaves mantidas pelo Android Keystore. O backup do Android está desativado.

O compartilhamento ocorre somente quando o usuário escolhe Exportar e seleciona um aplicativo de destino. Depois disso, o aplicativo escolhido passa a tratar o arquivo segundo seus próprios termos de privacidade.

Assinaturas salvas permanecem no aparelho até serem excluídas pelo usuário, até que os dados do Easy Sign sejam apagados ou até a desinstalação. Arquivos temporários de exportação ficam no cache e podem ser removidos pelo Android.

Para dúvidas sobre privacidade, entre em contato com a QED pelo e-mail qed.xyz.yt@gmail.com.
