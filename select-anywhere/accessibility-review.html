Accessibility Service demonstration

This video demonstrates the Select Anywhere Accessibility disclosure and consent flow, manual service enablement, explicit activation through Android’s Accessibility entry point, a temporary text-selection session, copying the selected result to local History, and service disablement.

Select Anywhere is a productivity utility and is not presented as an accessibility tool for disability support. The shipping interaction does not keep a permanent floating trigger over other apps.
